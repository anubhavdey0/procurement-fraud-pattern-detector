A lead is a reason to open the tender files. It is not a finding.

This page lists groups of bidders whose pattern of bidding is unusual under a stated statistical test. Unusual patterns have ordinary causes, and each lead lists the ones to rule out first. Nothing here says that any firm, person or official broke a rule.

Firms, tenders and buyers appear under labels such as FIRM-0042. The key that maps labels back to the public records stays with the analyst who ran the screen.

How far to trust these screens

Each screen was run on six sets of tenders in which a competition authority or a court identified a cartel. The number is the AUC: the chance that a cartel tender ranks above a competitive one. 0.5 is a coin toss. Below 0.5 the screen points the wrong way.

The variance screen is the most dependable of the seven. It separates cartel tenders (AUC of 0.6 or more) in {{ checks.n_cv_works }} of {{ checks.n_cases }} cases and fails in the rest. Screens that need bid amounts cannot run at all on a source that publishes only awards.

Method

Firms and tenders form a bipartite graph: a bid is a link between a firm and a tender. Two firms are linked when they bid on at least {{ config.min_joint }} tenders together and that is more than independent bidding would give (false discovery rate {{ config.fdr }}). Groups are grown on those links. Four screens are then applied to each group.

ScreenWhat it asksFires when
{{ screen_labels.closed }} Is the group's bidding mostly with itself? After Wachs and Kertesz (2019). Exclusivity of {{ config.exclusivity_min | f2 }} or more, and higher than in {{ config.n_null }} reshuffled markets (p at most {{ config.alpha }}).
{{ screen_labels.rotation }} Do members take turns winning? A runs test on the sequence of winners, with a mid-p. More changes of winner than chance, at a false discovery rate of {{ config.outcome_fdr | f2 }}. Needs {{ config.rotation_min_wins }} wins.
{{ screen_labels.cover }} Does a member keep bidding and keep losing? Wins against equal odds. A member with {{ config.cover_min_bids }} or more bids wins a quarter or less of the expected number, at a false discovery rate of {{ config.outcome_fdr | f2 }}.
{{ screen_labels.price }} Are the group's awards closer to the estimate than tenders from the same buyers that drew as many bidders? Stratified rank test (van Elteren), at a false discovery rate of {{ config.outcome_fdr | f2 }}. Needs {{ config.price_min_awards }} compared awards.

Every threshold is a judgement call and can be changed. Bidders are matched by name, so two people who share a name are merged and a renamed firm is split.
